How auto-play works

Auto-play just clicks "Step ▸" for you on a timer — every step is the same table lookup, stack push/pop, or match you'd get stepping manually, it just happens on its own so you can watch the stack and input change. Click Pause at any point to stop and continue by hand, or lower the speed to "Slow" to read each action before it moves on. It stops automatically once the parse accepts or rejects.
